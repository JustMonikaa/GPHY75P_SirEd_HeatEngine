Piston Stroke: ADIABATIC EXPANSION
Speed:
Stage 1 • Hot Reservoir Pipe
Hot Boiler Stage (TH)
Isothermal / Isobaric Heat Addition (+QH)
High-temperature heat source energizes the gas: molecules turn hot red and accelerate to high speed (high pressure).
Stage 2 • Piston Engine
Work Output Stage (Piston-Cylinder)
Adiabatic Expansion
Piston is currently pushed OUT (Work +Wout): volume increases (molecules spread apart), pressure & temperature drop.
Stage 3 • Cold Reservoir Pipe
Cold Sink Stage (TC)
Isothermal / Isobaric Heat Rejection (−QC)
Waste heat is rejected to the cold sink: molecules cool to icy blue and slow down (low pressure).
Stage 4 • Return Compression
Return Feed Stage
Adiabatic Pressurization & Return
Gas is compressed back into the high-pressure boiler zone: intermolecular distance shrinks and molecular velocity rises.
Molecular Indicators Across Pipes & Piston:
• Color (Temperature): Bright Red/Orange (Hot TH) → Cool Blue (Cold TC)
• Velocity (Pressure): Fast movement in High-Pressure pipe → Slow drift in Low-Pressure pipe
• Distance (Volume): Closely packed when compressed → Spread far apart when expanded

Compressor Speed:
Stage 1 • Mechanical Compressor
Work Input Stage (+Win)
Adiabatic Compression
Compressor work input (+Win) squeezes low-pressure vapor into a tight volume: molecules pack closely together, speed up (high P), and turn hot red.
Stage 2 • High-P Condenser
Warm Surroundings (TH)
Isobaric Heat Rejection (−QH)
Hot high-pressure refrigerant releases heat QH to the room: molecules remain closely packed and fast while cooling from red to warm orange/yellow.
Stage 3 • Expansion Valve
Throttling Choke (Isenthalpic)
Adiabatic Throttling Expansion
Fluid passes through narrow restriction: pressure drops abruptly, molecules spread far apart (volume expands), slow down, and chill to icy blue.
Stage 4 • Low-P Evaporator
Refrigerated Cold Space (TC)
Isobaric Heat Absorption (+QC)
Cold, widely spaced, low-pressure refrigerant absorbs heat QC from inside the refrigerator before returning to the compressor intake.
Refrigeration Loop Molecular Behavior:
• High-Pressure Top Branch: Closely spaced molecules (low V), fast velocity (high P), Hot Red → Warm Orange
• Low-Pressure Bottom Branch: Widely spaced molecules (high V), slower velocity (low P), Icy Blue → Cool Cyan
